# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone who interacts with this project, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints  
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully  
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the portfolio quality  

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks  
- Public or private harassment  
- Publishing others’ private information (doxxing) without permission  
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting  

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors they deem inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
